Settings

TMDB API key

Your CSV contains only titles and dates. Genres, languages, countries and runtimes come from TMDB — free, instant, no card. Without a key the app falls back to TVMaze, which covers series only. The key is stored in this browser and sent only to api.themoviedb.org.

Metadata cache

Exporting lets you keep your enriched metadata across browsers and machines without re-fetching.

Data

What this can and cannot know

Netflix's export has two columns: title and date. There are no timestamps, so hour-of-day stats are impossible. There are no durations, so every hours figure is an estimate built from TMDB/TVMaze runtimes multiplied by episodes watched. Netflix also logs a row once you watch enough of something, not for partial views, so totals skew slightly low.
